About

👋 I am currently a final-year Ph.D. Student at State Key Laboratory of Internet of Things for Smart City, University of Macau (UM), advised by Prof. Zhenning Li. Previously, I received my Bachelor's degree from Chang'an University and my Master's degree from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). Before joining UM, I was also fortunate to be mentored by Prof. Ruimin Ke and Prof. Ruey (Kelvin) Cheu. My current research interests lie in Autonomous Driving, World Model, and LLMs. On these topics, I have published 30+ papers in top-tier journals, including IEEE TPAMI and TR Part C, as well as at leading AI conferences such as CVPR and AAAI, with several selected for oral presentations.
